Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to your common questions

How to place an order?
It's very simple — choose your favourite book, then click the WhatsApp or Telegram button. Talk to us directly and confirm your order.
How long does delivery take?
After order confirmation, delivery generally happens within 3–7 working days. It may take a little longer depending on your location.
How to make payment?
UPI, Bank Transfer and Cash on Delivery are available. Payment details will be shared when you place your order on WhatsApp.
Can I return or exchange a book?
If the book is damaged or incorrectly delivered, send a photo on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or refund.
Do you deliver all over India?
Yes, we deliver all across India. Delivery charges may vary by location — confirm on WhatsApp.
How to check if a specific book is available?
Just send the book name on WhatsApp or Telegram — we will instantly confirm whether it is available or not.
Are bulk orders possible?
Yes! Special discounts are available for bulk orders. Contact us on WhatsApp and let us know the quantity.

Have more questions?

Talk to us directly — we are always available to help
