Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

Last updated: January 2025

Introduction

Shree Ji Online Book ("we", "our") takes your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our website or services.

What Information We Collect

When you contact us or place an order, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Phone number (via WhatsApp/Telegram)
  • Delivery address
  • Order details (which book you want)

We do not directly collect any sensitive financial information.

How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used only for the following purposes:

  • Processing and delivering your order
  • Providing updates about your order status
  • Providing customer support
  • Confirming delivery

Do We Share Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with any third party. Only your address is shared with the courier service for delivery purposes — nothing else.

WhatsApp & Telegram

When you contact us via WhatsApp or Telegram, those conversations fall under the respective platform's privacy policy (WhatsApp/Telegram). We use those conversations only for order fulfillment.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to keep your information secure. We do not store your data unnecessarily and delete sensitive details after the order is complete.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
